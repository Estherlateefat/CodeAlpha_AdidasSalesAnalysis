# CodeAlpha_AdidasSalesAnalysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship.

The project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales data using Python to identify important sales patterns, trends, and business insights.

The analysis covers:

- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data cleaning
- Business question development
- Data visualization
- Sales performance analysis
- Business insights and recommendations

## Tasks Completed

### Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis

The objective of Task 2 was to explore and understand the Adidas sales dataset.

The analysis included:

- Understanding the structure of the dataset
- Checking the number of rows and columns
- Identifying missing values
- Checking for duplicate records
- Cleaning numerical and financial columns
- Examining descriptive statistics
- Identifying potential outliers
- Analyzing sales by product, region, retailer, and sales method
- Answering important business questions

### Task 3: Data Visualization

Task 3 focused on transforming the analysis into meaningful visualizations.

The following visualizations were created:

1. Total Sales by Product
2. Total Sales by Region
3. Total Sales by Retailer
4. Total Sales by Sales Method
5. Monthly Adidas Sales Trend
6. Units Sold vs Total Sales
7. Total Sales by Product and Sales Method

## Dataset

The dataset contains Adidas sales transactions with information such as:

- Retailer
- Retailer ID
- Invoice Date
- Region
- State
- City
- Product
- Price per Unit
- Units Sold
- Total Sales
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin
- Sales Method

## Key Findings

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

- Men's Street Footwear generated the highest total sales among the products.
- The West region recorded the highest total sales.
- West Gear recorded the highest total sales among the retailers.
- In-store sales generated the highest total revenue.
- Online sales recorded the highest number of units sold.
- The analysis identified unusually high sales and unit values that require further investigation as potential outliers.
